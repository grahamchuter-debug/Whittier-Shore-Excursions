Whittier cruise and excursion season runs late spring through early fall, with Prince William Sound boat operations peaking when cruise ships call. Month choice shifts wildlife activity, daylight and crowd pressure at the tunnel.

May-June

Longer daylight, cooler conditions and fewer tunnel traffic jams than midsummer peaks.

July-August

Peak cruise demand, strongest whale activity and busiest booking window for sound cruises.

September

Shoulder season with softer light, fewer ships and often good value on extensions.

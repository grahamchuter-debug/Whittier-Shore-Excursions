Prince William Sound is one of Alaska's great marine wilderness areas — a maze of fjords, rainforest islands and active tidewater glaciers reachable within hours of Whittier. For cruise passengers, this is the reason to choose Whittier over a pure transit stop.

Boat routes vary by operator and conditions, but most cruises combine glacier viewing passes with wildlife scanning for whales, sea otters and seabird colonies. Morning departures from Whittier typically offer the smoothest sea state.

  • ✓Wildlife cruises emphasise marine mammals and birdlife across longer routes.
  • ✓Glacier cruises focus on calving faces and blue ice in protected channels.
  • ✓Blackstone Bay offers a more remote glacier experience for repeat visitors.

Prince William Sound from Whittier delivers consistent marine wildlife — humpback and orca sightings, rafts of sea otters, harbor seals and dramatic seabird colonies along glacier-fed coastlines.

  • ✓Whales: humpbacks and orcas frequent sound channels; timing varies by month.
  • ✓Sea otters: common in kelp beds and protected coves.
  • ✓Seabirds: puffins, kittiwakes and bald eagles on cliff colonies.
  • ✓Land wildlife: mountain goats and bears appear on some scenic and trail routes.

Whittier's story is unlike any other Alaska town. Built rapidly during World War II as a secret military port, most residents once lived under one roof in the Begich Towers — a fourteen-story building that still houses condos, offices and the post office.

Walking the waterfront reveals rusted rail infrastructure, tunnel mouths and mountains that trap sound and weather. Understanding this context deepens a Prince William Sound visit beyond checking off a glacier cruise.

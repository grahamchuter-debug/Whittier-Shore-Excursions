Whittier sits at the head of Passage Canal, reached through the Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel. Most cruise operations stage at the port terminal with tour pickups coordinated around tunnel traffic windows.

Where Ships Dock

Cruise ships use Whittier's deepwater terminal at the edge of town. The compact layout means harbor, tour offices and the tunnel portal are all within a short transfer.

Tunnel Logistics

Road access runs on a timed schedule through North America's longest highway tunnel. Confirm whether your tour includes tunnel coordination and allow buffer on self-drive days.

Currency

US dollars. Cards widely accepted; keep cash for small vendors.

Weather

Prince William Sound is cool and wet even in summer. Waterproof layers are essential on boat days.

Wildlife Season

Marine mammals are active May through September with peak whale sightings in midsummer.
